Rotate root protection

Take and verify an encrypted key-store backup first. Keep its old recovery key separately: an old backup still needs the old key. This operation does not re-encrypt documents.

Restore a sealed recovery copy

Creates a new sealed copy in the operator-configured recovery directory. It does not replace the running store. Independent backup and clean-machine evidence are still required before retiring old-root snapshots.
